Skin personalities

Three personalities behind data-ui-skin. Dark neutral surfaces can't lean on lightness alone — near black, tiny L steps are imperceptible — so each separates its stacked surfaces its own way: Default and Elevated add a depth-varying --neg-chroma tint over a lifted base, while Carbon stays purely neutral and lifts its dark base high for real lightness gaps. Read the <form> as the check.
