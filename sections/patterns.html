Compositions of the primitives — no new CSS, no new class. Each is bare elements arranged by the rules already shown; reach for these before hand-rolling layout.

Modal form

<form popover class="menu">

The <form> itself is the popover — .menu.glass, no wrapper. A .spread title-and-close header, fields on the form, and a single-row <fieldset> footer. It's plain HTML (no framework): it anchors under its trigger, light-dismisses, and the frost blurs the page behind — click Add to open it:

click to open · X or Cancel closes it · the frost blurs the page behind

Header — .spread, not .split

title · close

.spread keeps both children their natural size and pushes them to the edges — the title hugs the left, the close button stays square on the right. .split forces two equal halves, stretching the icon button into a giant block.

Footer — one row

<fieldset>

Wrap every action in a single <fieldset>: the fields row rule puts them side by side, each taking an equal --row-width share. One compact row keeps the whole form visible — no full-width button stack pushing content off small screens. Weight the primary wider with --row-width.

tokeneffect
<fieldset>All buttons onto one wrapping row
--row-widthA button's share of the row (default 1 = equal)
orderSecondary first, primary last (right)
 
<!-- equal split -->
<fieldset>
  <button type="button" class="sec">Cancel</button>
  <button type="submit" class="pri suc">Save</button>
</fieldset>

<!-- primary weighted wider -->
<fieldset>
  <button class="sec" style="--row-width:1">Cancel</button>
  <button class="pri suc" style="--row-width:2">Create &amp; link</button>
</fieldset>

The shell is interchangeable — put popover on the form to anchor it under a trigger, or make it a <dialog class="modal"> to center on a scrim; .glass is optional either way. The title + close header and single-row footer stay constant.
